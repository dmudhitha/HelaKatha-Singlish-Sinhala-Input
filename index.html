Core Capabilities

Engineered for Seamless Sinhala Input

⌨️

Phonetic Transliteration

Type naturally in Singlish (e.g., amma, lanka) and watch it convert to flawless Sinhala (අම්මා, ලංකා) in real-time with comprehensive phonetic rules.

🛡️

Zero Clipboard Pollution

Never overwrites or interferes with your system clipboard. Employs direct OS scancodes, Win32 Unicode, and IMK protocols across all operating systems.

🔤

502 UCSC FM Abhaya Rules

Full support for visual-order FM Abhaya ASCII output for Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, and legacy publishing software.

🧠

Self-Learning N-Gram Engine

Adapts to your unique typing style. Learns words and sentence bigrams dynamically so your most frequent words always appear at #1.

⚡

Strict UI Auto-Hide

Candidate suggestion boxes strictly hide upon Space, Enter, or punctuation, keeping your screen clean until your next word begins.

🔒

100% Client-Side Privacy

Zero telemetry, zero external network requests. All transliteration logic executes entirely on your local machine.
